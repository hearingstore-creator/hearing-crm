Digit Recorder

Records the spoken digits for the Digits-in-Noise test. Each recording is trimmed and level-matched automatically, then saved as 1.wav, 2.wav … for the digits/en or digits/bn folder.

  • Use one speaker for every digit, ideally a clear, calm voice (a staff member is perfect).
  • Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings. A wardrobe full of clothes works well.
  • Hold the phone or microphone 15–20 cm from the mouth and keep that distance the same.
  • Say each digit naturally and in the same even tone, as if reading a list. Don't stress or stretch it.
  • Press Record, wait half a second, say the digit, then press Stop. Re-record any take you don't like.

Recordings stay in this browser until you download them. Put the files in public/hearing-test/digits/<language>/, replacing the placeholder MP3 files (delete the old .mp3 files, or WAV will not be picked up). Then re-norm the score cut-offs as described in the README.
