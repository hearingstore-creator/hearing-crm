1The big picture

New→ Tele-counselled→ Assessment→ Trial→ Decision→ WON = Sale
From any stage a deal can be closed as LOST (with a reason).
LeadThe person – name, phone, hearing details. One person = one lead, forever.
DealOne buying chance. A repeat customer coming back = a new deal on the same lead.
ActivityThe next thing to do: call, WhatsApp, visit or task.
Golden rule: never leave an open deal without a next activity. If it has none, it will be forgotten.

3Office hours & holidays

  • Office time: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM. Weekly holiday: Friday. Public holidays are added in Pipeline Settings.
  • First call target: Website lead → 15 minutes; Quick Add / manual lead → 1 hour (office time).
  • A lead arriving at night, on Friday or on a public holiday is due at 10:00 AM + target on the next working day.
  • You can still plan work on a holiday or after hours – the system only shows a warning note, it never blocks you.

4Daily routine (Call Centre)

  1. Open Leads → Deals Board.
  2. Click My Day – the badge shows how many tasks are due. Work Overdue first, then Today, then Tomorrow.
  3. Call the customer from your mobile phone.
  4. Back in the CRM, tap the result of the call on that activity (table below).
  5. If the customer moved forward, drag the deal card to the next stage.
  6. Repeat until My Day is empty.
ResultWhat the system does automatically
No answerPlans the next call in 2 office hours
BusyPlans the next call in 30 office minutes
Call backAsks you for the time, then plans the call (warns if holiday / after hours)
InterestedPlans a follow-up call the next working day
Not interestedSuggests marking the deal Lost
Wrong numberSuggests marking the deal Lost
After 3 "No answer" in a row the system warns you and suggests Lost. You decide.

5Adding a lead

A. Quick Add – fastest, while on the phone

  1. Deals Board → Quick Add.
  2. Enter Name and Phone; optionally hearing loss per ear.
  3. Save – a deal opens in New with the first call due in 1 office hour.

B. New Lead – full form

  1. Leads → New Lead.
  2. Fill name, phone, district, source and interest. Age is optional.
  3. Choose the starting stage. A later stage asks for that stage's required information (section 6).
  4. Save.

C. Website leads

Arrive automatically as New deals with a 15-minute first call. Nothing to type.

The same phone number cannot be saved twice. If the customer already exists, open the existing lead – a new deal is created for the new enquiry.

6Moving a deal through stages

Drag the card to the next column. A small box asks only for what that stage needs:

StageMust enter before movingRotting after
New–1 day
Tele-counselledInterest (what they need) + counselling note7 days
AssessmentAppointment date or tick "Walk-in"7 days
TrialTrial product + trial return date10 days
DecisionQuoted value (price)7 days
A card shows a rotting warning when it stays in one stage longer than the days above.

7Closing a deal

Won

Press Won on the deal (or it closes as won when the sale is entered for this customer). The lead becomes a customer.

Lost

Press Lost → choose a reason: Price Bought elsewhere No response Not ready Medical referral Distance Other → optionally, lost to whom. A lost deal can be Reopened later.

Other actions

  • Transfer – send to another branch / owner.
  • Reassign – change the responsible person.

8Lead profile (All Leads → click a name)

  • Personal info, hearing loss per ear and the Lead Journey (stages reached, Won/Lost).
  • Communication timeline – SMS, past follow-ups, appointments.
  • Activities card:
    • Pending activities – red = overdue, amber = today.
    • Call / WhatsApp → tap the result chips (section 4). Visit / Task → Done.
    • Bottom form: type, date/time, person, note → Add. Tick Already done to log past work. Choose Note to only write a note.
  • No open deal? Open or reopen a deal from the Deals Board first.

9All Leads list

  • Stage cards on top show live counts – click one to filter.
  • Search by name or phone (type at least 2 characters).
  • Import (if permitted) – upload leads from Excel.
  • Export and Delete – Super Admin only. Every export and delete is recorded in the Activity Log.

10Pipeline Settings (Admin)

  1. Stages – rename, colour, probability, rotting days, required fields.
  2. Lost reasons – add or edit.
  3. Office hours – set opening / closing time and tick weekly holiday(s) (default Friday).
  4. Public holidays – pick date + name → +. Remove with the delete icon.

All changes are logged.

11Reports (Managers)

  • Deal Reports → Speed to lead – was the first call on time (office-hours target)?
  • Response time by staff – who calls fastest.
  • Conversion and lost reasons.
  • Website Leads – overview of web enquiries.
  • Lead Funnel (Reports menu) – how many move from stage to stage.

13Daily checklist

  • Start the day with My Day.
  • Clear red (overdue) first.
  • After every call, tap a result – never leave it pending.
  • Customer moved forward? Drag the card and fill the box.
  • Every open deal has a next activity.
  • Closing? Use Won or Lost + reason – never just leave it.
