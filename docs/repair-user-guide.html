1. The 6 Sub-Menus in the CRM

The Repair module is organized into 6 dedicated screens under the Repair navigation drawer. Each serves a dedicated function in clinic operations:

1. Repair Pipeline

The daily operational Kanban Board and List view. Shows all active jobs across the 6 pipeline stages with real-time status counters.

Route: admin/repairs

2. All Repair Jobs

Full historical registry (Plan A mode). Search all past repairs, closed warranty records, delivered devices, and historical tracking logs.

Route: admin/repairs?mode=legacy

3. New Repair Job

The official intake wizard. Search patient by name or phone, validate device serial number, run automated warranty checks, and print job cards.

Route: admin/repairs/create

4. Quick Walk-in

Rapid 1-minute modal for same-day clinic servicing: wax guard replacement, dehumidification, acoustic tube change, and cleaning without lab dispatch.

Route: admin/repairs/quick

5. Supplier Reconciliation

Dedicated accounting screen. Reconciles monthly lab statements from manufacturers against clinic challans with one-click payment settlement.

Route: admin/repairs/supplier-reconciliation

6. Supplier RMA Stock

Manufacturer replacement stock & warranty replacement serial audit trail. Ensures supplier warranty units cannot be registered under duplicate serials.

Route: admin/repairs/rma

2. Dual Workflow Routing: In-House vs Lab Dispatch

When receiving a hearing aid, the system prompts you to choose the service path based on device diagnostic severity:

Patient Walk-in
Serial & Physical Check
Path Selection

Path A: In-House Service

When to use: Minor issues that the clinic audiologist can fix on site within 15 to 30 minutes.

  • Cerumen (wax) clogging the receiver spout
  • Damaged microphone protector / wax guard
  • Moisture extraction in vacuum dryer chamber
  • Earmold tubing replacement & acoustic seal test
Zero Lab Dispatch: The job bypasses Stages 2, 3 & 4 and moves straight to Ready for Pickup / Delivered with immediate cashier settlement.

Path B: Supplier Lab Dispatch

When to use: Major internal hardware faults, dead components, custom shell remakes, or manufacturer warranty replacements.

  • Dead receiver or blown amplifier module
  • Internal circuit corrosion or microphone failure
  • RIC cable break or custom shell cracking / re-shell
  • Official Manufacturer Warranty Claim (Signia, Phonak, Oticon, Widex, ReSound)
Full 6-Stage Tracking: Generated with courier tracking #, lab diagnostic quote, patient phone authorization, and supplier ledger reconciliation.

3. Financial Architecture & Anti-Double-Entry Protections

The Repair Module seamlessly ties physical device maintenance into the CRM financial core. Every transaction follows strict double-entry ledger rules:

Event / Step Trigger Screen Customer Ledger Impact Supplier Ledger Impact Anti-Duplication Guard
Advance Deposit Stage 3 or Show Page Credit (Money Receipt) No impact Locked against duplicate submission
Supplier Lab Memo Stage 5: Receive from Lab No impact Credit (Payable to Lab) Deduplication Engine: If bill ID or memo exists, row is updated rather than duplicated.
Final Handover Stage 6: Deliver & Collect Debit (Service Charge)
Credit (Final Payment)		 No impact 10s Rapid Click Lock: Prevents double-clicking cashier from charging customer twice.
Voided / Declined Job Stage 3: Decision (Void) Debit Inspection Fee (If applicable) No impact Supplier ledger is never credited for voided parts.
Accounting Golden Rule for Clinic Staff: Always enter the supplier's actual Cash Memo / Challan number when returning units from the lab in Stage 5. This ensures the monthly statement from Signia, Phonak, or Oticon matches down to the exact paisa during reconciliation!

4. Monthly Supplier Statement Reconciliation

Clinic payments to external manufacturer labs (e.g. Signia BD / Phonak) are usually paid grossly / periodically at clinic convenience, rather than paying each individual repair case by case.

How Accounts Reconciles Monthly Statements:

  1. Navigate to Repair → Supplier Reconciliation (admin/repairs/supplier-reconciliation).
  2. Select the Supplier Name (e.g. Sivantos Bangladesh) and the Billing Month.
  3. The system displays all lab repair jobs completed in that cycle with their respective Clinic Challan #, Job #, Serial #, Lab Memo #, and Billed Cost.
  4. Compare the CRM total with the physical monthly statement sent by the supplier lab.
  5. Click Bulk Settle & Issue Payment Voucher when issuing a gross bank check or electronic fund transfer. The system records the gross payment against the supplier ledger without needing to touch individual customer invoices.

5. Supplier RMA Replacement Stock & Audit Trail

When a manufacturer lab determines that an in-warranty hearing aid cannot be repaired and sends a Brand New Replacement Unit:

Complete Serial Chain Integrity

The CRM records a permanent Serial Chain Record. If Hearing Aid SN-10024 is replaced by SN-99051, both serials remain permanently linked in the patient's record.

Replaced Serial Protection

The original defective serial is automatically marked as Decommissioned / Returned to Factory in the database. Nobody in the clinic can accidentally re-sell or assign the old defective unit to another patient.

6. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

What if a patient refuses the repair estimate?

In Stage 3, choose Patient Declined (Void). The system cancels repair charges and immediately moves the job to Stage 5 (Ready for Pickup). The patient can collect their un-repaired device by paying only the standard clinic inspection fee (if applicable).

What if the barcode scanner does not read the serial?

Hearing aid serials are laser-etched and can wear off. You can search by the patient's mobile number, national ID, or Job Card Number. If the serial is completely worn off, verify it through the original sales invoice attached to the patient's CRM profile.

Can I switch between Board View and List View?

Yes! In the top toolbar of Repair Pipeline, toggle between Board View (visual Kanban cards across 6 columns) and List View (tabular spreadsheet view with sorting, export, and pagination).

Does syncing to accounts create double entries?

No. The deduplication engine automatically verifies existing invoice numbers and supplier memos. If you click sync multiple times or edit a repair cost, it updates the existing entry rather than adding a second ledger row.

What is the difference between Plan A and Plan B?

Plan B is the streamlined 6-stage workflow with automated tasks, courier waybills, and board cards. Plan A is the legacy registry kept for backward compatibility and raw database audits.

How do I handle emergency Quick Walk-in service?

Click Quick Walk-in on the top right. Select patient, check off service (Cleaning / Wax Guard), enter amount (e.g. ৳300), and click Instant Settle. The job completes in 30 seconds with immediate money receipt printing.
